Privacy Policy

Stock Book · Last updated: September 7, 2026

Stock Book ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal stock portfolio tracker for the Pakistan Stock Exchange. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control or delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID from Google Sign-In. This identifies your account; we do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Portfolio and financial data you enter — stock tickers, purchase/sale prices, quantities, dates, target prices, and any notes you add. This data is yours; we only store it so the app can show it back to you across your devices.
  • Price alert preferences — the target prices and tickers you ask to be notified about.
  • Push notification token — a device identifier (Firebase Cloud Messaging token) used only to deliver the price-alert notifications you've set up.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — if the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics automatically collects a crash report (stack trace, device model, OS version) to help us fix bugs. This does not include your portfolio data.

How your information is used

Your data is used solely to operate the app for you: displaying your portfolio, calculating gains/losses, sending the price-alert notifications you configure, and diagnosing crashes. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Where your data is stored

All account and portfolio data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), Google's cloud infrastructure. Data in transit is encrypted (TLS). Access to your data is restricted to your own signed-in account — the app's security rules prevent any other user from reading or writing your data.

Live market prices

To show live stock prices, the app reads a shared, publicly-sourced price feed that is not tied to your personal account or identity in any way.

Your choices

  • You can review and edit any portfolio data you've entered directly in the app at any time.
  • You can turn off notifications for individual price alerts, or all notifications, from your device's notification settings.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — see Account & Data Deletion for how.

Children's privacy

Stock Book is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change, and material changes will be reflected in the app's release notes.
