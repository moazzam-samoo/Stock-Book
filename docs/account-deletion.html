Delete Your Account & Data

Stock Book · Last updated: September 7, 2026

You can permanently delete your Stock Book account and all data associated with it at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Stock Book and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes your portfolio data, price alerts, withdrawal history, settings, and your sign-in account. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion without the app installed

If you no longer have the app installed, email us at Moazzamsamoo819@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign in (via Google Sign-In), with the subject line "Delete my Stock Book account". We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in information
  • All portfolio data — positions, buy/sell history, target prices
  • All price alerts
  • Withdrawal history and settings
  • Your push-notification device token
This action is permanent. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered. If you only want to stop using the app temporarily, simply sign out or uninstall the app instead — your data will remain intact if you sign back in later.
